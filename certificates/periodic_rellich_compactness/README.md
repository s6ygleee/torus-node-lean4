# discrete Rellich compactness on the bounded periodic branch

Fix the unit four-torus and let (a=L^{-1}). For a sequence of periodic
coexact lattice (r)-forms (u_L), use the standard Fourier interpolation
and assume the uniform energy bound
\[
\|u_L\|_{L^2}^2+\|d_a u_L\|_{L^2}^2+\|\delta_a u_L\|_{L^2}^2\le C.
\]
The Fourier symbol from the `periodic_d4_coercivity` record gives
\[
\widehat{\Delta_a}(k)=4a^{-2}\sum_\mu
\sin^2(\pi k_\mu/L).
\]
For every fixed (K), the modes (|k|\le K) form a finite-dimensional
subspace. For the high-mode tail, the energy bound gives
\[
\sum_{|k|>K}|\widehat u_L(k)|^2
\le C\,\sup_{|k|>K}(1+|k|^2)^{-1}
\le C(1+K^2)^{-1},
\]
after the usual Brillouin-zone identification. The finite low-mode part is
precompact and the high-mode tail is uniformly small; a diagonal argument
therefore yields an (L^2)-convergent subsequence of the interpolants.

Thus bounded periodic the finite completion coexact fields satisfy a self-contained
discrete Rellich compactness lemma. This closes the deterministic geometric
compactness consumer. It does not imply tightness of the interacting torus
measure, nor does it repair the unrestricted Poisson obstruction.
