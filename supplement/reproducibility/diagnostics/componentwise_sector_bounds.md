# Componentwise coframe, clock and Higgs bounds

For a coframe component with nonnegative lattice Laplacian,

\[
  H_e=m_e^2 I+\kappa_e\Delta\succeq m_e^2I,
  \qquad \lVert H_e^{-1}\rVert\le m_e^{-2}.
\]

At temporal stiffness `1.1876789651362785`, the five-state clock estimate is

\[
  (\log 5)^2+\frac{3}{4\kappa_t}=3.2217741720177973.
\]

For the Higgs radial variable,

\[
  \frac12r^4-2v^2r^2+2v^4=\frac12(r^2-2v^2)^2\ge0.
\]

These separate estimates do not establish a single convergent polymer
expansion for the coupled coframe-clock-Higgs measure.
