# Conditional source-to-continuum bridge

This record gives the conditional continuation used in the manuscript.  It is
not a proof that the literal Poisson source satisfies the hypotheses.

For an admissible completion sequence, assume uniformly in volume and cutoff

\[
H_{\Lambda,a}\succeq \kappa I,\qquad
\|R_n\|\le C\rho^n,\qquad \kappa>0,\quad 0<\rho<1.
\]

Then

\[
\|H_{\Lambda,a}^{-1}\|\le\kappa^{-1},\qquad
\sum_{n\ge0}\|R_n\|\le C/(1-\rho).
\]

The finite interaction calculation supplies a Hessian bound \(0.472\), a
coercive floor \(\kappa=0.528\), and
\(\rho=0.9825487365\), hence the inverse bound
\(1.8939393939\) and a finite geometric remainder.  These constants are
finite-completion data; they do not establish a source-uniform bound,
source pullback, or interacting ultraviolet estimate.  The continuation to a
distributional subsequence is therefore conditional on the displayed
uniform assumptions and on tightness of the field family.

The companion verifier checks the arithmetic margins and the geometric sum.
