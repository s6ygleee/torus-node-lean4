# Distributional continuum summary


For the massive Gaussian sector,
`lambda_a(n)=m^2+4 a^(-2) sum_mu sin^2(pi a n_mu) >= m^2+c|n|^2`.
Consequently, for every `s>1`, the `H^{-s}` second moments are uniformly
bounded.  The pinned clock satisfies
`sup_L E ||T_L-P_K T_L||_(H^{-s})^2 <= C_(s,kappa) K^(2-2s) -> 0`.
Finite infrared families are tight and equicontinuous in the verified window,
so the free/massive sectors admit subsequential distribution-valued sectors.

This does not prove interacting ultraviolet cutoff removal, uniqueness, or a
pullback to the unrestricted source.
