# Finite links, chiral matter, and Higgs/Yukawa covariance


Site-dependent links transform as `W'_yx=V_y W_yx V_x^(-1)` and plaquettes
transform by conjugation.  The quark link is
`W^(q)_yx=W^(3)_yx tensor W^(2)_yx`.  With
`tilde H=i sigma_2 H^*`, the SU(2) pseudoreality identity gives covariance of
the down-type Yukawa term.  Finite link residuals are below `6.5e-16` for
SU(3) and `4.7e-16` for SU(2); the four anomaly sums and three Yukawa charge
conditions are exact rational zeros.

The source-derived link, chiral projector, Higgs bimodule, and family
multiplicity remain open.
