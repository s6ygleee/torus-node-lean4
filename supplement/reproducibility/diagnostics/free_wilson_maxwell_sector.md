# Free Wilson--Maxwell sector

For a periodic \(L^4\) lattice, the free Wilson Maxwell symbol is

\[
\widehat\Delta_L(k)=4\sum_{\mu=1}^{4}\sin^2(k_\mu/2).
\]

It has one scalar zero at \(k=0\).  After the gauge zero mode is removed, the
transverse projector

\[
P^T_{\mu\nu}(k)=\delta_{\mu\nu}-k_\mu k_\nu/|k|^2
\]

satisfies \(k_\mu P^T_{\mu\nu}(k)=0\).  The finite Fourier verifier checks
\(L=2,3,4,6,8,12,16\), including the normalized covariance trace and the Ward
residual.  The Gaussian sector has a volume-uniform \(H^{-s}\) bound for
\(s>1\).

This closes only the free finite-volume Maxwell calculation.  The
interacting Wilson polymer estimate, its source pullback and the physical
photon statement remain open.
