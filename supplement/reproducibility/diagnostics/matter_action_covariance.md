# Finite matter-action covariance


For the tensor-product quark transport, the finite kinetic term is invariant
under the site-dependent link transformation.  The Higgs conjugate
`tilde H=i sigma_2 H^*` uses only SU(2) pseudoreality.  The finite fermion
link residual is below `4.5e-16`, and the up, down, and lepton Yukawa
covariance residuals are below `1e-12`.

This closes the finite local matter-action check only; a source-derived
derivation and a uniform interacting continuation are not included.
