On the deterministic periodic lattice \((\mathbb Z/L\mathbb Z)^4\), the
nonzero-momentum symbol is
\[
 \lambda(k)=4\sum_{\mu=1}^{4}\sin^2(k_\mu/2),
 \qquad
 \lambda_{\min}^{\rm coexact}(L)=4\sin^2(\pi/L).
\]
After removing harmonic modes, \(L^2\lambda_{\min}^{\rm coexact}(L)\to4\pi^2\).
The recomputation script and its output log are stored alongside this record.  The
result applies to the deterministic periodic branch only; it is not a theorem
for random Poisson geometry or an interacting continuum measure.
