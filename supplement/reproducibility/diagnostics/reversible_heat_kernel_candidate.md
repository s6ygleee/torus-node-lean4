# Reversible heat-kernel completion candidate

On the 24-element projective relation sector, choose three adjacent
transpositions as generators.  With adjacency matrix \(A\), define
\(L=3I-A\) and

\[
\mu_\beta(g)=[e^{-\beta L}]_{e,g}.
\]

The finite verifier checks normalization, inverse reversibility,
non-negativity and positive semidefiniteness of the convolution kernel.  These
properties hold on the tested finite sector.  A grid scan over
\(\beta\in[0,2]\) has minimum sampled total-variation distance
\(0.24415741655004386\) from the declared finite selector, at
\(\beta=0.265\).  This is above the sufficient threshold \(1/6\).

The measure is consequently an admissible optional completion, not the
canonical measure and not a consequence of the raw torus relation data.
Equality with the canonical selector and a source-derived choice of the
generators remain open.  The grid scan is a finite diagnostic, not a
no-go theorem for all possible measures.
