# All-volume Wilson state and the conditional massless pole

For the declared finite Wilson representative, the manuscript proves finite
Gibbs measures, reflection positivity, subsequential infinite-volume Gibbs
states and inheritance of reflection positivity by the limit.

The transverse massless pole is separate. Its continuation uses the Ward
identity together with signed Feshbach and connected-source estimates. The
finite ratios are recorded here, but the source-uniform nonlocal connected
bound has not been established. The pole statement therefore remains
conditional although the all-volume state theorem is proved under the stated
assumptions.
